# AgriCart - Agricultural Marketplace App

**AgriCart** is a comprehensive mobile marketplace platform designed to connect farmers, local buyers, suppliers, traders, and agribusinesses in the Philippines. The name "AgriCart" plays on the concept of "add to cart" (o bongga sino ka jarn!), making it easy and familiar for users to purchase agricultural products just like any e-commerce platform.

## What is AgriCart?

AgriCart is a Flutter-based mobile application that serves as a digital marketplace for agricultural products. It enables direct connections between agricultural producers and consumers, streamlining the buying and selling process for fresh produce, livestock, farm equipment, and other agricultural goods.

## Target Users

- **Farmers** - Sell their products directly to buyers
- **Local Buyers** - Purchase fresh agricultural products from local farmers
- **Suppliers/Traders** - Source products and connect with farmers
- **Agribusinesses** - Manage bulk purchases and inventory

## Key Features

### For Buyers
- Browse products by category, price, or location
- Search and filter products easily
- Add products to cart and checkout seamlessly
- Chat or call sellers directly
- Track orders in real-time
- Rate and review sellers

### For Sellers (Farmers)
- Create and manage seller profile
- Post product listings with photos, prices, and quantities
- Manage inventory and sales
- Receive notifications for orders and inquiries
- Access sales analytics (top products, revenue trends)

## Product Categories

- Fresh Produce (vegetables, fruits)
- Livestock & Poultry
- Seeds & Fertilizers
- Farm Tools & Equipment
- Processed Goods (honey, dairy, meat products)

## Communication

- In-app chat and messaging system
- Push notifications for updates and inquiries

## Payment Options

- Cash on Delivery (COD)
- Mobile wallets (GCash, Maya)
- Bank transfer integration via PayMongo

## Logistics & Delivery

- Connect with partner couriers for delivery services

## Security

- Verified user registration (via ID or phone number)
- Secure authentication and data handling

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend**: Flutter (Dart) with BLoC architecture
- **Backend**: Django REST API
- **Database**: Firebase Firestore
- **Image Storage**: MongoDB GridFS
- **Payments**: PayMongo integration
- **Notifications**: Firebase Cloud Messaging

## Mission

AgriCart aims to modernize agricultural commerce in the Philippines by providing a user-friendly platform that bridges the gap between farmers and consumers, supporting local agriculture and making fresh, local products more accessible to everyone.
